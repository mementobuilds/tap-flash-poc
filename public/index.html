How to play

Two tiny games. Pick one and go.

Tap Flash is hold-and-release reflex timing. Split Fifty is all about cutting a shape as close to 50:50 as you can. No setup, no account, just play.

Tap Flash

Press and hold through the dark phase, then release when TAP appears.

Split Fifty

Drag the dotted line left or right, then lock in your cut and see how close you got.
