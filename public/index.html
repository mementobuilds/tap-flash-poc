Welcome

What is Tap Flash?

Tap Flash is a tiny browser reaction game, but the real point is the loop behind it. Players leave feedback, the Tap Flash Pi Agent reads it, memento approves changes, the agent implements them, and the game redeploys.

How to play

Pick a mode: Tap Flash is hold-and-release reflex timing, and Split Fifty is all about cutting a shape as close to 50:50 as you can.

Why it exists

The goal is to prove an AI agent can participate in a real build → feedback → iterate loop on a live product, not just make a reaction game.

How Tap Flash works

Play it, break it, leave feedback, and watch the next version land.

  1. Choose a mode: Tap Flash or Split Fifty.
  2. In Tap Flash, press and hold, then release when TAP appears.
  3. In Split Fifty, drag the cut line and try to split the shape as close to 50:50 as possible.
  4. Tap Flash early releases add a +100 ms penalty.
  5. Each mode runs for 5 rounds and tracks its own score.
  6. If your score cracks a live board, add your 3-letter name.
  7. This game is part of a continuous product loop: users leave feedback, the Tap Flash Pi Agent reads it, memento approves changes, and new versions get shipped live.
  8. Want to help shape it? Jump to the VCL feedback tab and leave your thoughts there.
